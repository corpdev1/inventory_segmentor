Observations

  • There are 6 cases where the anomaly is a large spike up. The displayed anomaly is also exactly at the top of the spike. Such cases are easy.
  • There are 5 cases that are level shifts (up or down).
  • When a level shift happens, we may show anomalies to the user which are not exactly at the extreme of the level shift. The anomaly may be marked somewhere on the transition region. Thus, to make such test-cases future proof, we should accept anomalies that are detected on the transition region.

Skyline

Reference: https://github.com/etsy/skyline

Each algorithm raise or not an anomaly. The final score is the average of the outputs of all algorithms.

Constants

FULL_DURATION = The considered duration stored for analysis

Median Absolute Deviation

A timeseries is anomalous if the deviation of its latest datapoint with respect to the median is X times larger than the median of deviations.

Algorithm :

  • Compute the median of the timeseries : Median = median(Series)
  • Compute the absolute difference between the timeseries and the Median : Demedianed = absoluteValue(Series - Median)
  • Compute the median deviation : MedianDeviation = median(Demedianed)
  • If the latest point is 6 time larger than the median, raise an anomaly Demedianed[-1]/MedianDeviation > 6

First Hour Average

Calculate the simple average over one hour, FULL_DURATION seconds ago. A timeseries is anomalous if the average of the last three datapoints are outside of three standard deviations of this value.

Algorithm :

  • Consider the series from the beginning to Now - (FULL_DURATION - 1 hour) Series = Series[:Now - (FULL_DURATION - 1 hour)] (if FULL_DURATION = 1 Day, we consider the last hour of one day ago)
  • We compute the mean and standard deviation Mean = mean(Series), StdDev = std(Series)
  • If the average of the last three datapoints if outside the three std we raise an anomaly T = mean(Series[-1]+Series[-2]+Series[-3]) absoluteValue(T - Mean)>3*StdDev

A timeseries is anomalous if the absolute value of the average of the latest three datapoint minus the moving average is greater than three standard deviations of the average. This does not exponentially weight the MA and so is better for detecting anomalies with respect to the entire series.

Algorithm :

  • We compute the mean and standard deviation Mean = mean(Series), StdDev = std(Series)
  • If the average of the last three datapoints if outside the three std we raise an anomaly T = mean(Series[-1]+Series[-2]+Series[-3]) absoluteValue(T - Mean)>3*StdDev

Standard Deviation from Moving Average

A timeseries is anomalous if the absolute value of the average of the latest three datapoint minus the moving average is greater than three standard deviations of the moving average. This is better for finding anomalies with respect to the short term trends.

  • Take the exponentially weighted moving average (EWMA) of the timeseries EwmaSeries = EWMA(Series)
  • Compute the exponentially weighted moving standard deviation (EWSTD) of the timeseries EwstSeries = EWSTD(Series)
  • If the last point of the series minus the average is outside 3 std deviation we raise an anomaly absoluteValue(Series[-1] - EwmaSeries[-1]) > 3 * EwstSeries[-1]

Mean Substraction Cumulation

A timeseries is anomalous if the value of the next datapoint in the series is farther than three standard deviations out in cumulative terms after subtracting the mean from each data point.

Algorithm :

  • Compute the difference between the timeseries and the mean of the timeseries except the last point Series = Series - mean(Series[:-1])
  • Compute the standard deviation of the series except the last datapoint StdDev = std(Series[:-1])
  • If the absolute value of the last datapoint is outside 3 Std we raise anomaly absoluteValue(Series[-1]) > 3 StdDev

Least Squares

A timeseries is anomalous if the average of the last three datapoints on a projected least squares model is greater than three sigma.

Algorithm :

  • Fit a linear model of by the component of the timeseries (time in seconds, values) ProjectedValues = M*Time + B
  • Compute the error between the projected values and the actual values Error = Values - ProjectedValues
  • Compute the standard deviation of the error values ErrStdDev = std(Error)
  • If the average of the last three datapoints if outside the three error std we raise an anomaly T = mean(Series[-1]+Series[-2]+Series[-3]) absoluteValue(T) > 3*ErrStdDev

Histogram Bin

A timeseries is anomalous if the average of the last three datapoints falls into a histogram bin with less than 20 other datapoints

Algorithm :

  • Compute the average of the last three points T = mean(Series[-1]+Series[-2]+Series[-3])
  • Compute the histogram of the Series h = histogram(Series)
  • If the average of the last three datapoints belongs to the bin with less than 20 points we raise an anomaly

Client feedback session @September 7, 2022

After client call we got the following feedback:

  • The most important features to consider while alerting on the LF incident are: log volume and duration
    • Short spikes (less than 5 min) are not interesting
    • Incidents that last for more than 5 minutes should be sent to the client
  • Clients are interested to know about the incidents having both metric and log count anomalies from the same component
    • They usually assign a WARNING label to such issues
    • They downgrade the severity of these incidents only in case of maintenance or when they know that some changes had been done on their side (manual intervention)
  • Based on client feedback, there is no clear correlation between log severity and the label for incidents having both metric and log count anomalies from the same component

Based on the feedback above we could derive the following LF rules:

  • Duration- based:
    Info: "if (log frequency > 0.5) and (duration <= 181.0)"
Warning: "if (log frequency > 0.5) and (duration > 181.0) and (duration <= 300.0)"
Critical: "if (log frequency > 0.5) and (duration > 300.0)"
  • LF incident type- based:
    Warning: "if (log_frequency_and_metric_from_same_component_affected > 0.5)"
Critical: "if (log_frequency_and_metric_from_same_component_affected > 0.5) and (duration > 300.0)"

Concept shift detection

The goal of this experiment is to test concept drift detection by ADWIN algorithm from river package.

We used data of Zerogachis log counts during the period when one of our agents failed and stopped sending data.

Conclusion:

  • Concept drift detection could be applied for time series with usually the same values observed shape
  • Concept drift detection showed poor results for noisy time series with random behaviour: random noisy shape
  • This type of anomaly could be used for info alerting (as a pop-up message) since it is prone for massive alerting

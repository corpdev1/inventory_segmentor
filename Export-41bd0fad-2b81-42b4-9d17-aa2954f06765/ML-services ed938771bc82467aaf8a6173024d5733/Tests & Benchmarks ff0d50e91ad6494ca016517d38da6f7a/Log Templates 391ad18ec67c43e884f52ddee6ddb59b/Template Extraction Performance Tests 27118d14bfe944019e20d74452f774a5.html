Why drain in not good enough on HealthApp ?

We notice that drain outputs way too many templates and consequently a bad loss value.

This can be due to the nature of logs:

setTodayTotalDetailSteps=1514038440000##7008##548365##8661##12456##27174269
setTodayTotalDetailSteps=1514038440000##7009##548365##8661##12551##27174951

Drain fails to predict one template for these two log lines.

Drain vs Logan: Conclusion

From the results we obtained, we can see that Logan and Drain are pretty close to each other in terms of accuracy. However, Logan seems to have greater generalization potential and works decently on a wider range of log types, typically because it starts with little to no assumptions compared to Drain , that suffers from structural problems that were emphasised on the Toy dataset, mainly due to the strong assumptions on which it is built. Logan's ability to generalize however, comes with a cost: execution time. In that area Drain is much faster. This can be addressed by implementing a distributed version.

Which one to chose?

Depends on the nature of the logs.

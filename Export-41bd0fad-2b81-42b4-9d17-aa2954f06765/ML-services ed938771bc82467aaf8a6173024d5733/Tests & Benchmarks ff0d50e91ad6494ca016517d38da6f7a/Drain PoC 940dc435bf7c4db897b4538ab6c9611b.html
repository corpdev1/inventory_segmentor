Conclusion

PoC was stop due to the results

Drain performs better than IBM Drain due to the masking function.

We add more masking instructions in our Drain based on our data (logs from clients)

We don’t need IBM Drain, but some idea should be take into account.

  • extra_delimiters: delimiters to apply when splitting log message into words (in addition to whitespace).
  • parametrize_numeric_tokens: whether to treat tokens that contains at least one digit as template parameters.
  • add prefix and suffix to masking ( NUM become <:NUM:>) make more readable the template and the masking part

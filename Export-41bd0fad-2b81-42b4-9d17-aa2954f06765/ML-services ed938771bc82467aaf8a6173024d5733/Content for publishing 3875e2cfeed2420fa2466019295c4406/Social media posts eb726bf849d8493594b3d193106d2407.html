Feature Highlight - Log dashboard

Wanna have a beautiful visual summary of the 20M log lines your stack generated yesterday?

Sure,

  • select sources of data (your favorite services for example)
  • take a quick look on logs but don’t try to read all of them. You can’t
  • click “Patterns”. Somebody grouped your logs for you by pattern
  • Errors and warnings? filter in
  • what the heck is this “internal server error”?! zoom in to see the problematic logs
  • done

Feature Highlight Plug and play observability - the coffee cup test bla

Deploy PacketAI in less than 60s? 🕰  let’s go

  • Login
  • Go to: Deploy PacketAI → Agent
  • Pick your OS
  • Copy link, paste on terminal (do it for as many hosts as you want)

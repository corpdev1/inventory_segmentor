@Isaac Cohen Sabban wrote :

Approach :

  • Remove the numbers from logs
  • Compute the Embedding matrix from scratch
  • Work on the HDFS 100K logs
  • Split train and test : take the x first lines for train, where x belongs to[5000, 10000, 20000, 30000] and predict on [95000, 90000, 80000, 70000]
  • Compute the cosine similarity between all the logs
  • Construct a graph of similarities between the logs based on the max similarity
  • Extract the connected components from the graph and assign them to the same cluster
  • Compute the score between the ground truth and the computed clusters

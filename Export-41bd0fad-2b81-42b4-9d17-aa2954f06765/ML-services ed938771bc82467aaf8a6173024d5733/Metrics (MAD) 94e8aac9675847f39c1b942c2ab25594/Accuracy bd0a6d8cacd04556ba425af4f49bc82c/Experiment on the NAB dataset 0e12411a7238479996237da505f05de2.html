We've evaluated MAD pipeline using the NAB dataset. As for July 27 our pipeline consists of two detectors: Walter and Skyline

We've been tracking 4 metrics: accuracy, f1 score, precision and recall. Our aim is to maximize the recall as we are tolerant to false positives at the anomaly detection stage.

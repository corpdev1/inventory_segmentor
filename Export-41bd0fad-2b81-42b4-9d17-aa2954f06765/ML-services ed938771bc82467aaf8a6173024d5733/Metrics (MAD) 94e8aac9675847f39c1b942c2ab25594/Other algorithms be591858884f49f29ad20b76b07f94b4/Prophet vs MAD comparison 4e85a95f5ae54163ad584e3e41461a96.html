Time

Univariate data

Prophet takes 1.46 seconds in average to produce one prediction during first 7 minutes of observations whereas MAD takes in average 0.0096 seconds for the same period of time.

During first 3 hours of observations Prophet takes 1.43 seconds in average to produce one prediction whereas MAD takes in average 0.014 seconds for the same period of time.

Multivariate data (10 metrics)

Since Prophet is not designed for multivariate data the amount of time required to provide one prediction increases linearly with the number of metrics. It takes 13.3 seconds in average to produce one prediction during first 7 minutes of observations for 10 metrics.

MAD is designed in the way to be able treating multivariate data and thanks to vector operations it takes 0.0102 seconds in average to produce one prediction during first 7 minutes of observations (which is the same time as for the univariate data)

Conclusion

Prophet is a great tool while applied to the univariate data with strong seasonal patterns over reasonably high period of aggregation. It is not suited for anomaly detection in streaming multivariate data of high frequency (our case) and requires an accurate and verified hyper-parameter tuning. It could be, however, used to detect anomalies for selected (= bounded) set of metrics when we do not have tight time constraints (1 hour / day / week / ... aggregations).

Some take-aways

  • The time-between model can be adjusted based on coefficient of variation, as described here.
    • This will help with anomalous regions that occur (nearly) periodically. e.g., periodic spikes.
  • The time-between model can be sensitive to presence of small amount of noise. For e.g., this rabbitmq metric. This could be addressed in a few ways:
    • Fit a multimodal distribution to the time-between samples. e.g., kernel density estimation.

      Requires time to experiment and validate.

    • Have a sliding window based forgetting of old samples. The noisy samples could be forgotten in due course.
  • Sliding window based fitting of samples will lead to more relevant deviation and time-between distributions.
  • Fitting a multimodal distribution will help with deviation samples also (requires time to experiment and validate).
  • The rules for duration could be tweaked according to the distribution of observed samples.
  • When less than five samples are available, the significance score can consider the observed deviation also, part from the duration that is already considered.

Explaining the clusters

iSQUAD paper proposes BCM (Bayesian Case Model) to compute for each cluster a subspace of features that identifies this cluster. It also computes a prototype of this cluster. This helps the user to understand the clusters and identify the root cause. As the above solution is not publicly available. I used as an alternative the decision tree classifier to identify the important features for the incidents classification

Instance mysql8f8fd6c89154d836

Dataset:

164 incidents of mysql (anomalies raised by MAD on mysql metrics)

Clustering

Clustering gives 2 main clusters (below) with similar symptoms (anomalous metrics detected by MAD). Anomalous metrics of Incidents in Cluster 2 are a subset of anomalous metrics of incidents of Cluster 1. This tells that both may have the same root cause but in different intensity.

Main symptoms

  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#misc-no_subject',
  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#data-no_subject',
  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#bytes#data-no_subject',
  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#read#ahead_evicted-no_subject',
  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pool#reads-no_subject',
  • 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#dirty'

Clusters

Cluster 1: ~50 incidents

  • Spike up of 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#dirty'
  • Level shift up of 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#read#ahead_evicted'
  • Spike down 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#misc'
  • Spike up of 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#data'

Cluster 2: ~30 incidents

  • Spike up of 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#dirty'
  • Spike up of 'mysql#status#innodb#buffer_pool#pages#data'

Other types of incidents are sparse

Comparison with slow query logs

In the period of these anomalies(164), there was 26824 slow queries. Only 323 are within 20 seconds of an anomaly raised by mad. Also for all anomalies, there was slow queries with latencies going to 18 sec. (10% slowest queries). The high number of slow queries without any anomaly may be explained by low resources, because the slow queries happened without any abnormal behavior of mysql.

Setting the regularization parameter

  • Regularization parameter of 50000 is not always enough for a time series of length 50000.
  • 20000 is enough for time-series of length 29000 (aggregation of 2 minutes).
  • 50000 is sufficient for system#diskio#iostat#write#per_sec#bytes-sda of linux6040eafa3a9e9f0012cebfd4. It is a sparse time series, with several spikes. Take a smaller time window and check.
  • 50000 captures the trend of system#memory#actual#used#pct-no_subject of linux6040eafa3a9e9f0012cebfd4 well.
  • But 50000 doesn't work for system#cpu#total#pct-no_subject of linux6040eafa3a9e9f0012cebfd4.
  • 50000 is enough for system#memory#actual#used#pct (linux6040f7e73a9e9f0012cec090).

Notes about the solver:

  • C solver is scalable: works for even 60K samples (aggregation over 1 minute windows of data in S3 leads to time-series with 60K samples).
  • Check the time series of length 59000 that took around 1.5 seconds.

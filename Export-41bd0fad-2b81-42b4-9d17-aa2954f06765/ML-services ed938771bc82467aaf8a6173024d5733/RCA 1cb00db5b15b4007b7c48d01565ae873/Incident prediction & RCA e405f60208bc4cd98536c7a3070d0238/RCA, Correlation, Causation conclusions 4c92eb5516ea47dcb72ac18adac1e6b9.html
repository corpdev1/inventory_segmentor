PoC on causality

Granger causality test

Requirements:

  1. Stationary
  1. linear continuation

Platforms:

  • Sockshop
  • Affluences

Tests on sockshop with system metrics and opentelemetry metrics:

We stress resources of containers and we compute causality test between opentelemetry metrics (request durations) and system metrics

For microservices that have big traffic like catalogue. Causality test identified only catalogue metrics as cause of the performance behavior. For other microservices, the causes are less accurate. These microservices have their request duration mostly constant with spikes.

steps:

  1. Compute sequence of patterns by autoencoders
  1. Compute similarity
    1. Cosine similarity

PoC on abnormal behaviour correlation (Anodot inspired)

steps:

  1. Encode time series to binary vector
    1. 0 is normal behavior
    1. 1 is abnormal behavior
  1. Hash binary vector by “Locality sensitive hashing”
  1. Compute similarity
    1. Cosine similarity
    1. Clustering with Latent dirichlet allocation

Other conclusions and results

On Affluences

  • Correlation:
    • 65 % of pairs of anomalies within 5 minutes have flux correlation higher than 0.6
    • For all pairs of highly correlated metrics, more than 80 % of their anomalies  do not happen within a short window
  • Causality:
    • Pairs of anomalies that happened within 5 minutes: 191
      • 65% have granger causality p value less than 0.05 with maxlag at 5
    • 25% anomalies on a metric A are followed (or simultaneously) by an anomaly on a metric B such that A gcause B.
      • Proportions are different between different metrics

NB: We consider Anomalies raised by mad after noise reduction.

Possible applications of Correlation and Causality

  • Anomaly 1 raised on A

    If we receive anomaly 2 on B with a 5 minutes window,

    And fluxcorr(A,B)>0.6, group 1 and 2

    Repeat for every new anomaly

  • For anomaly raised on metric A, show top N predecessors based on granger causality

What exactly are sparse time-series? Is the following description acceptable?

  • The time-series takes low values most of the time with occasional spikes.

Doubts

  • Should all or majority of the low values be (close to) zero or low w.r.t. the spikes?
    • e.g., system#diskio#iostat#write#per_sec#bytes-sd*, system#network#out#bytes-no_subject, Docker-1 (docker#diskio#summary#rate-no_subject): the low values are low but not zero.
  • What should be the difference in magnitude between the low regions and the spikes, when compared to the values of the spikes?
    • Linux-2 (system#memory#actual#used#pct-no_subject) in one or three hour window.
    • Linux-2/-3/-4 (system#cpu#total#pct-no_subject): Peak values (corresponding to spikes) only 3 to 4 times the low regions. Also, the low regions are not (close to) zero. There is a daily periodicity to the low regions (which has been detected so far based on 1 day MWA).
  • Sparsity depends on the time window over which the time-series is analysed.
    • system#diskio#iostat#write#per_sec#bytes-sda (appearance of sparsity depends on the time window: larger (2 days or 3 hours) than smaller (1 hour)). Because a few big spikes are enough to give a time-series a sparse appearance.
  • Should a spike be of 1 (10 second) sample duration?
    • Linux-1: system#diskio#iostat#read#per_sec#bytes-sdc

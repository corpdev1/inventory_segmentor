This is a time-series with three time periods (approx.) half a day, (approx.) one day and a lunar month.

The data can be downloaded in a per month basis from this link:

https://www.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/waterlevels.html?id=8443970&units=standard&bdate=20200801&edate=20200831&timezone=LST/LDT&datum=MLLW&interval=6&action=data

Choose the time range, aggregation period of 6 minutes and click on "Data Only", followed by "Export to CSV".

Note: only the data in the column 'Verified (ft)' has been considered for obtaining the time-series for our experiment. Ensure that this column has data before downloading it.

Anodot's ACF based approach

There is a dominant cluster for a time period of 125 samples (12 hours 30 minutes). ACF based approach is not able to capture the time periods of (approx.) 24 hours and lunar month.

ACF+PSD approach

One can see three fitted quadratics (in magenta in the bottom middle figure) corresponding to periods of approx. 12 hours, approx. 24 hours and also, (approx.) 16 days are having a strong concave shape.

We take a closer look at these cases below,

First, we test the 12 hour candidate on the original ACF. Next, we low pass filter the time-series and test the 24 hour candidate. We can see that both the time period candidates are on top of a concave curve with the correct width. Subsequently, the ACF+PSD approach is catching a longer time period of around 16 days. Even though, we can see why based on the ACF of the filtered time-series.

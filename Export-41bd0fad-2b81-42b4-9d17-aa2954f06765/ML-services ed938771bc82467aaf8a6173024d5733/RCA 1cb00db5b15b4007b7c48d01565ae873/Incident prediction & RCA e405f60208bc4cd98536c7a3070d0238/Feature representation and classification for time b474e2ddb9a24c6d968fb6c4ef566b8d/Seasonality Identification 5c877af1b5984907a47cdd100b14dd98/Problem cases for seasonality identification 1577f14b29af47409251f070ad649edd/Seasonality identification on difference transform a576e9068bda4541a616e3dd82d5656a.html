Seasonality identification on difference transformed time-series

@Hariprasad Kannan wrote:

The Docker-2 component here has periodicities missed because of trend in the time-series.

Three settings were tried:

  • Computing the 1-hour MWA of the original time-series and computing the difference time-series.
  • Computing the difference on the original time-series and applying the 1-hour MWA on the difference time-series.
  • Computing the 1-hour MWA of the original time-series, computing the difference and applying the 1-hour MWA on the difference time-series also.

Observations:

  • Of the three, the third setting gave the best results.
  • The first approach produced a very noisy time-series that led to poor results. The second approach leads to more visually noticeable periodic behavior in the time-series but doesn't improve the results.
  • In general, taking difference leads to more high frequency behavior in the resulting time-series. This leads more noisy ACF, with more small peaks that lead to false positives.

Docker-2 has 24 time-series with 18 periodic and 6 non-periodic cases.

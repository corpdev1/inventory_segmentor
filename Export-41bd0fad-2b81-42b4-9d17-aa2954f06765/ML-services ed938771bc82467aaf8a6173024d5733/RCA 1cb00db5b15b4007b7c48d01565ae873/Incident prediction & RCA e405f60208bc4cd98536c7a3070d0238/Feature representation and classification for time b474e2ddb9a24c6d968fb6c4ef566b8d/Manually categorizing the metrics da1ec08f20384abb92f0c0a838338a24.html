There are some time-series that can be readily grouped into clusters. However, there are many which cannot be classified readily because of noise. In order to take things forward, an agglomerative approach is being taken. First many clusters are created to group the time-series according to their more distinct behaviors. Then we will see whether some of these distinct clusters can be grouped together.

Observations

  • Many of the metrics are made of spikes. Different classes are characterized by differing density of spikes, regular or irregular time occurrence of the spikes, regular or irregular values of the spikes, presence of different levels of noise in-between the spikes.
  • In the case of 1 minute aggregation, it was difficult to categorize some time-series. They had to be observed in multiple time windows (6 hours, 1 day and a few days) to better understand their behavior.
  • There are a few metrics in RabbitMQ and MySQL that take discrete values. The shapes of the time-series are in the form of spikes or plateaus, where spikes are more common than plateaus.
  • There is a multiscale nature to the time-series made of spikes. In a smaller time window the density and the values of the spikes will make the time-series appear to belong to one class, while a larger time window will make it appear as a different (but related) class.
  • When the values of the spikes are similar, MAD algorithms adapt to these values and show anomalies only for spikes with a different value.

Notes about difficult cases

Possible reasons why we are not getting good enough performance

  • Limited number of ground truth anomalies.
  • There are case where it is difficult to decide:
    • What the anomalies are.
    • What the shape class is.
  • Magnitude of anomalies are different across time-series belonging to the same class.
  • There are eight detectors in hand of which seven (belonging to Skyline) are relatively simple. Also, there is similarity in behavior among a few of the detectors in Skyline.
    • It is not clear whether optimal weights will considerably pull up the performance.

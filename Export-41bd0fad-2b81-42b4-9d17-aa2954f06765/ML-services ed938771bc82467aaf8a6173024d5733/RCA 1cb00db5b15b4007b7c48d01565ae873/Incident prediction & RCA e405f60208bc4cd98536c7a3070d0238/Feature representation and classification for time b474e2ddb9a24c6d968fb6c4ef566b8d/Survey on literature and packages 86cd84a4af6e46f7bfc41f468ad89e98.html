In the Packet AI system, feature extraction and classification can be used for:

  • Clustering for RCA.
  • Selecting optimal weights for MAD. This may also involve offline clustering into representative groups of time series.
  • Compressed storage.

The following two references lead to others:

  • "A Review of Unsupervised Feature Learning and Deep Learning for Time-Series Modeling" by M Längkvist, L Karlsson, A Loutfi, Pattern Recognition Letters, 2014, 818 citations.
  • "Deep learning for time series classification: a review" by Fawaz et al, Data mining and knowledge discovery, 2019, 561 citations.
    • https://github.com/hfawaz/dl-4-tsc

Note: Techniques which are not tied to deep learning are labelled as "BEYOND-DL".

Time-series features can be extracted from individual time series or can relate two or more time series. Some approaches for feature extraction that relate groups of time series could be used for clustering alone.

(The works listed immediately below are from collaborating teams in Australia, France and UK.)

Time-series clustering

Alignment

Alignment, i.e., finding corresponding points between two time series (using dynamic time warping etc) etc appears as an intermediate step in several approaches for time series analysis.

  • "A Complexity-Invariant Distance Measure for Time Series", Batista et al, ICDM 2011, 314 citations. BEYOND-DL.
    • Unofficial: https://github.com/PeteWe/ts_similarity_tensorflow
  • Global alignment kernel
    • http://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/398402_abe1a0343a4e4e03977de8f3791e96bb.html
  • "Soft-DTW: a Differentiable Loss Function for Time-Series", Cuturi and Blondel, ICML 2017, 153 citations.
    • https://github.com/mblondel/soft-dtw
    • https://tslearn.readthedocs.io/en/stable/gen_modules/metrics/tslearn.metrics.soft_dtw.html
  • "Diffeomorphic Temporal Alignment Nets", Weber et al, NeurIPS 2019.
    • https://github.com/BGU-CS-VIL/dtan
  • "Temporal Transformer Networks: Joint Learning of Invariant and Discriminative Time Warping", Lohit et al, CVPR 2019.
    • https://github.com/suhaslohit/TTN
  • Survey: "Experimental comparison of representation methods and distance measures for time series data", DMKD 2013.

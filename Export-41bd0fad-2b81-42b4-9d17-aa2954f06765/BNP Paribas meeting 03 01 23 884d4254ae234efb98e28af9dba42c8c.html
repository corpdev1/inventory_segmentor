    • Alert on pattern drifts (Log Pattern Alerting - LPA)
    • Search logs more efficiently (LogManagement) on BNPP log data
  • NEXT: Future capabilities proposal. Grow vertically with AI on top of logs or laterally with metrics + AI for troubleshooting metrics + logs

    • AI: Troubleshooting V2 (AI-powered RCA with training on past issues)
    • AI: Detect patterns in metrics (MetricPatterns)
    • AI: Predictive analysis for capacity and performance (identify issues before they occur)
    • AI: Resource allocation optimization (ensure resources are well-utilized)
  • AI: on-premise technical integration
    • Need open source LLMs, no remote API calls permitted (to be confirmed by JMG)
    • Vanilla LLM are good but key point is LLM fine-tuning on specific domain

      • EleutherAI’s GPT-J : 6 Bn params - less Q than GPT-3

      • XLNet - easy to fine-tune
      • HuggingFace - tons of open source models

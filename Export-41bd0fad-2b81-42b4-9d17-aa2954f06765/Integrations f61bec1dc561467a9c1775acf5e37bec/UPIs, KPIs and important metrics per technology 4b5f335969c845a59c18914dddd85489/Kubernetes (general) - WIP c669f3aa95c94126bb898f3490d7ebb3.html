Kubernetes (general) - WIP

For best practices: https://www.datadoghq.com/blog/eks-cluster-metrics/

  • note that these metrics are from general Kubernetes and should work for EKS
  • only the metrics listed bellow are subject to MAD
  • only the highlighted metrics are high level KPIs (or SLIs) i.e: metrics that generate alerts
  • many metrics are not clear how they behave, further tests are needed

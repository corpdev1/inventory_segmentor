Affluences 12.04.2022

Description:

During the client call that took place on the 20th of April Affluences communicated us a production issue they faced on the mysql-8 on the 12th of April around 22h30 which was not reported by PacketAI. After internal investigation we can see that indeed our alerting system have not raised any incident alerts during mentioned period as well as during some days after the incident despite of numerous alerts that were detected. This incident silencing could be caused by the maintenance works that were performed prior to the client incident.

Root cause:

There are multiple reasons of non-alerting the user about the incident happened on the 12th of April. They include: noise reduction part silencing anomalies (SIRE-p, pertinent alerting rules and the defined unimportant metrics) as well as a potential data pipeline issue (we stopped receiving data between April 12th 21h and April 13th 7h).

  • SIRE-p was probably affected by the cluster maintenance works (according to @Rama Rao Ganji , transferring from 3 AZ to 1 took place prior to the incident timestamp). As a result we got data outliers in anomaly arrival times that affected the SIRE-p score used to adjust the MAD anomaly score. As a result the final adjusted scores did not reach critical level to exceed the threshold. Detected by MAD anomalies but reduced by SIRE-p:
    • system diskio iostat busy-sda on linux604b83a14c58ea0011d6220f on the 12th April at 22h40
    • mysql status handler read first on mysql6d7613c72a46df40 on the 12th April at 22h40
  • Some anomalies were wiped out because of pertinent alerting rules or unimportant metrics:
    • system cpu total norm pct on linux604b83a14c58ea0011d6220f on the 12th April at 22h40
    • system cpu total pct on linux604b83a14c58ea0011d6220f on the 12th April at 22h40
    • mysql status handler read first on mysql6d7613c72a46df40 on the 12th April at 22h40
    • mysql status queries on mysql6d7613c72a46df40 on the 13th April at 9h20
    • log frequency on mysql6d7613c72a46df40 on the 12th April at 22h50
  • Cluster maintenance works of moving aws ec2 instances from 3 AZ to 1: 90% of MAD pods were restarted 7 days ago (13th of April) however no MAD updates were pushed to prod since March 22nd. and according to Rama it could correspond to the time of cluster maintenance works
  • Possible data pipeline issues related to logstash (based on the alerts raised on the packet-infra-alerts channel). After discussing with @Marin Radman it was confirmed that no ml anomalies were sent by logstash to Minima (it explains the absence of MAD anomalies between 9 and 13 April on the chart above). Possible mitigation actions:

  • Address SIRE-p affected after data flow interruptions: while being a strong anomaly reducer it is prone to false negative alerts after any interruption of data flow (cluster stops, data pipeline breaks etc).
    • Assign less importance to SIRE-p → could increase the number of incidents
    • Add the logic of handling outliers caused by restarts: ideally we would like to know when an interruption took place and use this knowledge in order to reduce the effect of potential data outliers on the noise suppression part
  • Suppress pertinent alerting rules implemented by @Marin Radman : there is no longer need after we have introduced the Alerting Manager- every pertinent alerting rule could be imported there
  • Raise incidents based on SIRE abnormal regions: while being a strong alerting signal we do not fully take advantage of this feature and use it just as a coloring tool for abnormal regions on the Minima charts. Since the AM introduction we could control the level of alerts raised and sent to the user by introducing the corresponding business rules on the AM part
  • Review the list of important KPIs per technology: since the last time we defined this list we introduced a lot of changes to our product, made it more robust and designed a set of tools to effectively manage the false positives alarms. As a consequence we could consider extending the list of important KPIs (by adding network, disk metrics, log frequency, negative logs etc...) that would raise incidents as an option in order to trade between False positive and Negative rates.

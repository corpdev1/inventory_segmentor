ManoMano Architecture.

Overview:

  • Size of Infra: 800 servers, 11Tb of logs per month, equivalent of 8B log lines. They were hesitant to split data and send a sample to Packet as it involves special work for them.

  • Agents: They do not want to use agents on their machines directly for logs. Raw logs currently transit through their aggregator (based on https://www.fluentd.org/) where they tag the logs, filter all sensitive information like passwords etc.
    • DD works with a tagging system. Packet for the moment cannot as our agent does this “tagging” and maps the infrastructure.
    • We would explore this as one of our future integrations.
    • For the PoV, ManoMano would have made an exception by installing PacketAI agents on 10 key machines - something that they dont do for security purposes.

  • Transit Costs: Currently they pay 10K euros of NAT costs per month for all the traffic transiting through their infra. Plus 10K to Datadog for their services.
    • If they had to re-install agents and redirect traffic to PacketAI - it would cost them money to support this additional/duplicate traffic (As packet needs an agent to function and we would duplicate the information flow)

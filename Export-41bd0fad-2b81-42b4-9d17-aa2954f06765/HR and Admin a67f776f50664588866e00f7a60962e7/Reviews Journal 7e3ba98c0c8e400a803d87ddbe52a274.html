    Dengke, Yakov, Marin, Valentin

    1. variable metric length of Docker metrics leading to an issue for MAD. Solution: Dengke to build a logstash filter averaging container metrics before feeding them to MAD. No dashboard update needed
    1. as of today, we do not have a mechanism to remove zombie components (especially processes and services) from the master topology, hence from the whole backend.

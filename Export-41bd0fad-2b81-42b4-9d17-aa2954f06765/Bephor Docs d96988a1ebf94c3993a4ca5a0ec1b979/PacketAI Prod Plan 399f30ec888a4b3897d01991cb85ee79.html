Why this plan was chosen

  • we are disparate for client interaction, feedback and data
  • allows us to iterate fast on business value: features and ML algos

Requirements

  • client expectation management, and communication clarity
  • manage the financial aspect (free trial, and because it might be unstable → free for three months)

Risks

  • client not happy with the unstable service
  • repetitive manual work for the team but in short cycles

Affluences monthly report 23-11-2021 WIP

Incidents and anomalies stats

Contrary to the usual, the past month (23 oct to 22 nov), we have seen multiple incidents on Affluences

  • we have generated 62 incident alerts with an average of 2 per day
  • of the 62 incidents, 60 were evaluated by the user
  • of the 60 evaluated, 25 were confirmed (41%), distributed as follows:
    • 3 critical incidents
    • 11 warning (could generate bigger incidents)
    • 2 debug (helps troubleshoot)
    • 9 Info (good to know about)
  • we fired 35 false positives, or 1.16 FPs/day on average (with one week that skewed the data because of logstash issue)
  • of the 35 FPs, 26 are from docker.diskio.read/write.service_time metrics (already addressed with new pertinent alerting rule so expected to disappear)

  • in the same period, traditional thresholds generated 128 alerts, distributed as follows:
    • 16 alerts of severity 1
    • 112 alerts of severity 3

Recap from video and notes:

Current Monitoring Solution: They don't use DD due to costs. Hence they use prometheus with https://thanos.io/ and ELK with https://www.elastic.co/guide/en/elasticsearch/client/curator/current/index.html. As Metron deploys 35-45 containers per client (as they are single tenant) they can't afford DD and hence they have lot of data as well.

  1. Tech stack is covered by us. MSK metrics can be covered using our metricbeat cloudwatch integration. Logs using Lambda
  1. They have a single tenant infrastructure - so for the PoC we can monitor one client - confirm data volumes
    1. Overall they have 90 hosts (all environments), 4 days of metrics data = 200GB. Hence month should be around 1Tb and a lot of log volume.
    1. They broke down Loki with too many logs and scrapper did not work as too many logs. Too many pods (5-6K pods) and too much volume - double problem. He had 3 prom cluster with 60GB of RAM but wasn't able to start
    1. 3 FTEs (Devops/SRE's) in the Infra team with Gilles as team lead. 4 architecture teams with 5-6 dev in each team.

Pain points:

  1. Pain to manage open source - prom for metrics and elk for logs
    1. Correlation between logs and metrics
  1. As it is a single tenant, clients have completely different behaviours. One client may send 5k time series in a few seconds and one may send 200 every minute. Thus thresholding is super difficult as thresholds are not standard. Thus ML can be very helpful.
  1. Not a lot of incidents but RCA is a problem as teams have to work together to do RCA. Takes time.
